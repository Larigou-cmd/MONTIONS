# Metformina: prompts das 8 cenas no Google Flow

Configuração: Omni 1.1 Flash, 720p, 10 s, 9:16, x1 (15 créditos cada, 120 no total).
Os rótulos e as datas entram depois, no Remotion. Por isso os prompts pedem "no text".

**Estilo, repetido em todos os prompts:** hand-drawn stick figure animation, black ink lines on warm cream paper, slightly wobbly line, only accent color is soft lilac purple, simple and funny, vertical 9:16, no text, no letters.

## 1. Gancho
Hand-drawn stick figure animation, black ink lines on warm cream paper, slightly wobbly line, only accent color is soft lilac purple, simple and funny, vertical 9:16, no text, no letters. A single round white pill sits in the center of the paper. A thin green stem sprouts out of the top of the pill and grows upward, leaves unfold, and it blooms into a cluster of small lilac pea-like flowers (goat's rue, Galega officinalis). The camera slowly pushes in on the flowers.

## 2. Idade Média
Hand-drawn stick figure animation, black ink lines on warm cream paper, slightly wobbly line, only accent color is soft lilac purple, simple and funny, vertical 9:16, no text, no letters. A medieval village with a thatched hut. A stick figure peasant with a straw hat drinks an entire wooden bucket of water in one go, then runs comically toward a bush, then comes back and drinks again. A stick figure peasant woman with a headscarf picks lilac flowers from a field and drops them into a cauldron, making tea, and hands the peasant a steaming cup.

## 3. 1914, a galegina
Hand-drawn stick figure animation, black ink lines on warm cream paper, slightly wobbly line, only accent color is soft lilac purple, simple and funny, vertical 9:16, no text, no letters. An early 1900s laboratory. A stick figure chemist with round glasses and a mustache boils lilac flowers in a glass flask over a flame; the liquid turns into small white crystals in a test tube. A line chart on the wall goes down. Then a lab mouse that tasted the crystals turns pale, wobbles dizzily and falls on its back with X eyes, comically. The chemist slaps his forehead.

## 4. 1922, Dublin e o esquecimento
Hand-drawn stick figure animation, black ink lines on warm cream paper, slightly wobbly line, only accent color is soft lilac purple, simple and funny, vertical 9:16, no text, no letters. Two stick figure chemists in lab coats assemble a molecule made of ball-and-stick atoms, proudly holding up a small white pill. Suddenly a giant glowing vial of insulin slides in under a spotlight and everyone turns to look at it, cheering. The little pill is pushed into a dusty drawer, the drawer closes, cobwebs grow over it and calendar pages fly away.

## 5. Filipinas
Hand-drawn stick figure animation, black ink lines on warm cream paper, slightly wobbly line, only accent color is soft lilac purple, simple and funny, vertical 9:16, no text, no letters. A tropical clinic with palm trees outside the window. A stick figure patient with a scarf and red nose sneezes loudly. A stick figure doctor with a white coat and stethoscope gives him a small white pill. A blood sugar gauge needle on the wall goes down. The doctor rubs his chin thinking, then a lightbulb appears above his head.

## 6. 1957, Jean Sterne
Hand-drawn stick figure animation, black ink lines on warm cream paper, slightly wobbly line, only accent color is soft lilac purple, simple and funny, vertical 9:16, no text, no letters. Paris, a simple Eiffel Tower drawn in the window. A stick figure French doctor with a bow tie opens a dusty drawer, blows the dust off a small white pill and smiles. The pill grows a cute little mouth and happily chomps sugar cubes one by one, crumbs flying, like a hungry little monster.

## 7. Os primos
Hand-drawn stick figure animation, black ink lines on warm cream paper, slightly wobbly line, only accent color is soft lilac purple, simple and funny, vertical 9:16, no text, no letters. Three cartoon pills with little arms and legs stand side by side on a stage like a family photo. The two pills on the left get stamped with a big red X and fall over backwards off the stage. The pill on the right stays standing, winks and gives a thumbs up.

## 8. Hoje
Hand-drawn stick figure animation, black ink lines on warm cream paper, slightly wobbly line, only accent color is soft lilac purple, simple and funny, vertical 9:16, no text, no letters. A simple drawn globe spins, and small white pills pop up on every continent. Cut to a small pharmacy counter where a smiling stick figure female pharmacist with brown skin, curly hair in a high ponytail, white lab coat with a neon green badge, hands a small paper bag to an old stick figure man, who smiles. Final shot: one white pill in the center sprouts a stem and turns back into a lilac flower.
